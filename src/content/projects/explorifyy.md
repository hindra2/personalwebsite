## About

Explorifyy is a mobile app that brings together events to help students find things to do on campus. The idea came from my freshman year, when my friends and I missed out on a lot of interesting events, so I wanted to build something that would make it easier to know what's happening around campus. I led a small team of friends to build it, and we shipped it to the App Store.

## Features

- A feed of events filtered by location and interest categories
- Hosting and posting your own events
- RSVPing to and saving events
- Sharing events with a deep link
